Scorpian VR

Scorpian VR is an open-architecture spatial computer running CachyOS Linux on x86_64 hardware. It combines Windows, Android, and traditional Linux applications with spatial computing through Proton/Wine, Waydroid, Stardust XR, and Monado. Built with developers in mind, Scorpian XR focuses on performance, compatibility, and deep system customization.

"Experience the future of VR with Scorpian VR."
